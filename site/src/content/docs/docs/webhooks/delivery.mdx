## Guarantees

| Guarantee | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **No change without its event** | The event is written in the same database transaction as the change it describes. If the change happened, the event exists. |
| **At least once** | Every event is delivered until you acknowledge it or retries run out. The same `eventId` can arrive more than once. |
| **Ordered by sequence, not by arrival** | Each subscription's events carry an increasing `sequence`. Deliveries can arrive out of order; apply the highest. |
| **Signed** | Every delivery carries `Payments-Timestamp` and `Payments-Signature`. |

## Retry schedule

A delivery fails when your endpoint answers anything other than `2xx`, does not answer in time, or cannot be reached. Each event gets up to **8 attempts**:

| Attempt | Sent after the previous attempt |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Right away |
| 2 | 5 seconds |
| 3 | 5 minutes |
| 4 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | 2 hours |
| 6 | 5 hours |
| 7 | 10 hours |
| 8 | 10 hours |

That covers about **27.5 hours** from the first attempt, long enough to ride out a deploy gone wrong or a night-time outage.

## After the last attempt

An event that fails all 8 attempts is **dead-lettered**: retries stop, the event and the last error are kept, and our alerting fires. Once your endpoint is fixed, we redeliver dead-lettered events. Because every event carries a full snapshot and a sequence, redelivery after a long gap still leaves your copy correct.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not fail on purpose">
  Answer `2xx` for an event you choose to ignore. A non-2xx answer is a failure and the event will be retried for a day.
</Aside>

## Timeouts

Answer quickly. Verify the signature, save or queue the event, and return. Do slow work, such as emails or provisioning, after you answer. A delivery that does not answer in time is treated as failed and retried, even if your handler finishes later. That is one more reason to deduplicate by `eventId`.

## Ordering in practice

Two events for the same subscription can arrive in either order, for example when the first delivery failed and was retried after the second one succeeded.

| Arrives | sequence | Stored sequence | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `charge.failed` | 4 | 3 | Store. Now 4. |
| `charge.succeeded` | 6 | 4 | Store. Now 6. |
| `subscription.past_due` (retried) | 5 | 6 | Ignore. Older than what you have. |

Your copy ends at sequence 6, which is the true current state.

## Monitoring your endpoint

- Alert on a rising rate of `400` from your webhook route. It usually means a wrong secret or a body parser change.
- Log `eventId`, `type` and `sequence` for every delivery you accept.
- If you have been down for longer than the retry window, tell us. We redeliver what was dead-lettered.
