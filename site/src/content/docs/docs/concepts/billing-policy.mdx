Each application has its own billing policy. We set it with you at onboarding. The defaults follow common practice for consumer subscriptions.

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `checkoutTtlHours` | `12` | How long an `incomplete` subscription waits for payment before it is canceled with `checkout_expired`. |
| `resumeAllowed` | `true` | Whether a cancellation can be taken back before the period ends. When `false`, resume answers `409 resume_not_allowed`. |
| `graceEntitlement` | `true` | Whether a `past_due` customer keeps access while we retry. Reflected in `entitled`. |
| `dunningScheduleDays` | `[1, 3, 7]` | Days after the failed renewal at which each retry runs. The last entry is the final attempt. Must have at least one entry. |
| `timeZone` | set at onboarding | The IANA time zone whose calendar sets billing dates, for example `Asia/Jerusalem`. |

## Choosing values

### checkoutTtlHours

Long enough for a customer who steps away to come back, short enough that abandoned checkouts do not pile up. Before canceling an expired checkout we ask the provider whether it was paid, so a slow payment is never lost.

### graceEntitlement

| Value | Customer experience | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `true` | Access continues while retries run. Most declines recover without the customer noticing. | Consumer apps, where losing access feels punitive |
| `false` | Access stops at the first decline and returns when a retry succeeds. | Expensive services where usage costs you money |

### dunningScheduleDays

More retries over a longer window recover more payments. A typical schedule spans about a week. Each retry is a real charge attempt on the card, so very frequent retries add little.

| Schedule | Retries | Last attempt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[1, 3, 7]` | 3 | 7 days after the failure |
| `[1, 2, 4, 7, 14]` | 5 | 14 days after the failure |
| `[3]` | 1 | 3 days after the failure |

## Changing a setting

Ask us. A change applies from then on:

- A new retry schedule applies to retries computed after the change. A retry already scheduled keeps its time.
- A change to `graceEntitlement` shows up in `entitled` on the next event or read for each subscription.
- The time zone is not changed after you have subscribers, because it would move their billing dates.

<Aside type="note">
  Policy applies per application. Two products of the same business can have different policies while sharing one card provider account.
</Aside>
