## Anniversary billing

Period one starts when the first payment succeeds. Every renewal falls on the same day of the month, or the same date each year, at the same local time. A customer who activates on the 14th at 09:31 renews every 14th at 09:31.

| Plan interval | Activated | Renewals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `month` | Mar 14, 09:31 | Apr 14, May 14, Jun 14 … at 09:31 |
| `year` | Mar 14, 2026 | Mar 14, 2027, Mar 14, 2028 … |

## Short months

When the anniversary day does not exist in a month, the renewal moves to the last day of that month. The original day is kept for the months that have it.

| Activated | Next renewals |
| --- | --- |
| Jan 31 | Feb 28 (Feb 29 in a leap year), Mar 31, Apr 30, May 31 |
| Jan 30 | Feb 28, Mar 30, Apr 30 |

## Your time zone

Billing dates follow the calendar of the time zone set on your application, for example `Asia/Jerusalem`. Two effects:

- A customer who pays at 00:30 local time on the 1st is billed on the 1st, not on the last day of the previous month in UTC.
- The local billing hour stays fixed across daylight saving changes.

All timestamps in the API and in events are ISO 8601 in UTC. Convert them for display.

<Aside type="caution">
  The time zone is set when your application is created. Changing it later would move every subscriber's billing date, so it is not done casually. Get it right at onboarding.
</Aside>

## When renewals run

- A renewal is due once `currentPeriodEnd` has passed. We never charge early.
- Due renewals are picked up every hour.
- While the charge runs the subscription stays `active`. It only becomes `past_due` on a confirmed decline, so a slow provider never interrupts access.
- The next period starts exactly where the previous one ended. A renewal that ran an hour late does not shift the customer's dates.

## The amount

The charge always uses the amount on the subscription's plan. Plans never change after they are created, so a subscriber is never repriced by accident. To raise a price, create a new plan for new subscribers. Existing subscribers stay on theirs.

## After a failed renewal

A successful retry pays for the period that failed. The new period starts at the original renewal date, so the customer's anniversary never moves and the grace days are covered. See [Handle failed payments](/docs/guides/failed-payments/).
